Our Story

A Cup Born from Kovilpatti's Soul

Nestled on the bustling Kovilpatti Main Road, Madras Masala Tea is more than a tea stall — it's a ritual. Every morning, as the town awakens, the aroma of freshly ground cardamom and simmering ginger fills the air.

We draw on generations of Tamil Nadu tea tradition. Our masala blend is ground fresh daily — a secret combination of seven spices that has earned us a loyal following across V.O.C. Nagar and beyond.

From the first sip to the last, we believe chai should be bold, warming, and deeply personal. Come as a stranger, leave as family.

The Menu
